Dreaming of Snakes

Snakes chiefly signal an enemy, slander and quarrels, though killing one promises victory.

●●●●● Mostly negative, with a happy outcome if you kill it

Depending on what happens in the dream

  • You see snakes: an enemy, slander, quarrels, even serious danger
  • A snake bites you: you will give in to bad influences and your business will suffer
  • You kill a snake: victory over enemies and success in love and work
  • A snake coils around you: you will be at the mercy of enemies

Depending on how you felt

  • Distrust: a sense that someone around you isn't genuine
  • Fear: an anxiety that grows the more you avoid it
