Dreaming of Absence

absence, departure, missing · ●●●●● mostly positive, with one shadow

Dreaming of absent people often foretells their return, stronger friendships and success.

What happens in the dream

  • You grieve someone's absence: sincere regret wins you lifelong friendships
  • You dream of absent people: they will soon return, and your business does well
  • Absent friends: news of them, good or bad
  • You are glad friends are absent: you will soon be rid of an enemy
  • They seem to stand by your bed: a worrying sign for them

How you felt

  • Longing: the dream often reflects the place a faraway person holds in your life
  • Nostalgia: it may express a wish to reconnect

Ask yourself

  • Who am I missing right now?
  • Is there someone I should get in touch with?

Source: G. H. Miller, Ten Thousand Dreams Interpreted, or What's in a Dream (1901); F. Fontaine, The Golden Wheel Dream-Book and Fortune-Teller (1862); A. H. Noe, The Witches' Dream Book (1885). Also searched as: departure, missing.
