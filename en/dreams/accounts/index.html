Dreaming of Accounts

accounts, bills, bookkeeping, invoices · ●●●●● mixed

Accounts in a dream speak of debts, disputes and sometimes a stroke of luck.

What happens in the dream

  • You find an error in your accounts: a piece of luck
  • Bills are presented to you: a risky position, perhaps a legal remedy
  • You balance accounts: a good sign; trouble at work and in love, then the respect of those around you
  • You pay them: a compromise in a serious dispute
  • You hold accounts against others: annoyances in your business
  • Ink-blotted accounts: a risk of illness

How you felt

  • Worry: the dream may reflect money concerns or things you must answer for
  • A need for order: it may express a wish to take stock

Ask yourself

  • Do I have scores to settle, in money or with someone?
  • What do I owe myself?

See also Debt · Ledger · Counting · Money · Addition

Source: G. H. Miller, Ten Thousand Dreams Interpreted, or What's in a Dream (1901); F. Fontaine, The Golden Wheel Dream-Book and Fortune-Teller (1862). Also searched as: bills, bookkeeping, invoices.
