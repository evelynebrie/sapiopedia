Dreaming of a Banner

banner, pennant, standard · ●●●●● mixed, depending on the banner's state

Your country's banner foretells victory or defeat depending on its state.

What happens in the dream

  • Your country's banner flies in a clear sky: triumph over your opponents
  • The banner is torn or battered: conflict and lost honors

How you felt

  • Pride: a need for belonging or recognition
  • Unease: a fear of seeing what you care about weaken

Ask yourself

  • Which group or cause do I feel I belong to?
  • What am I trying to defend at the moment?

See also Flag · Standard-Bearer · Victory · War · Soldiers

Source: G. H. Miller, Ten Thousand Dreams Interpreted, or What's in a Dream (1901). Also searched as: pennant, standard.
