Dreaming of a Bellman

bellman, announcer, herald, town crier · ●●●●● mostly positive, with one exception

The bellman announces good fortune and disputes settled amicably, unless he looks sad.

What happens in the dream

  • A bellman: good fortune is hurrying after you, and important disputes will be settled amicably
  • He looks sad: a sorrowful event or misfortune may follow

How you felt

  • Anticipation: you may be hoping for news
  • A need to be heard: a wish to announce something

Ask yourself

  • What news am I waiting for?
  • What would I like to announce to the world?

See also Bells · News · Message · Trumpet · Chimes

Source: G. H. Miller, Ten Thousand Dreams Interpreted, or What's in a Dream (1901). Also searched as: announcer, herald, town crier.
