Dreaming of Birds

birds, feathers, songbird · ●●●●● mostly positive, with some shadows

Birds are mostly a good sign, especially flying or singing, but wounded or fighting birds bring sorrow.

What happens in the dream

  • Birds with beautiful plumage: a favorable dream, with a wealthy, happy partner nearby
  • Flying birds: prosperity, and an end to what weighs on you
  • You hear birds singing: joy, and recovery for the sick
  • Molting, silent birds: cruelty toward the vulnerable
  • Birds fighting: adversity
  • Talking birds: admitting you lack the clarity a task demands
  • Young birds: news of a birth
  • A wounded bird: deep sorrow caused by a child
  • Birds fly over your head: enemies will harm you
  • You shoot birds: a poor harvest

How you felt

  • Freedom: the dream often reflects a wish to soar or escape
  • Light joy: an openness to beauty

Ask yourself

  • What would I like to fly away from?
  • What makes my day sing?
