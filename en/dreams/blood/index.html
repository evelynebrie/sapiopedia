Dreaming of Blood

blood, bloodstain, gore · ●●●●● mostly negative, with one happy exception

“Will all great Neptune’s ocean wash this blood
Clean from my hand?”

William Shakespeare, Macbeth, c. 1606

Dreaming of blood is unsettling, and the old dream books mostly read it as a sign of enemies, illness or bad luck. Today blood is usually linked to energy, injury and emotional strain: something that hurts, or something that drains you.

Summary: Blood mostly foretells enemies, illness and bad luck, with one happy exception.

What happens in the dream

  • You vomit blood while short of money: a sum of money will soon come, with good news of distant relatives, the only happy sign
  • Bloodstained clothes: enemies want to tear down a promising career; beware of new friendships
  • Blood on your hands: immediate bad luck unless you are careful
  • Blood flows from a wound: health worries and bad business

How you felt

  • Fear: the dream often reflects concern for your physical or moral wholeness
  • Guilt: a fear of being responsible for some harm

Ask yourself

  • What is hurting me right now?
  • Do I feel I have wronged someone?

What the old dream books said

G. H. Miller1901

Bloodstained clothes mean enemies trying to ruin a promising career; blood flowing from a wound, illness and worry; blood on your hands, bad luck unless you take care of your affairs.

F. Fontaine1862

The one happy exception: vomiting blood is good for the poor, who will soon receive money, and for the childless, who will have a child.

Read these as tradition, not prediction.

What it often means today

Blood is life force, and losing it in a dream is often read as losing energy, time or money to something or someone. A wound that bleeds tends to point to an emotional hurt that has not healed, especially if the dream comes after a conflict.

Blood on your hands is the image people remember most, and it has kept its old meaning of guilt. It often appears when you feel responsible for something that went wrong, even if the feeling is out of proportion. Bloodstained clothes, seen by others, suggest a fear of being exposed or judged.

For some dreamers blood has a very literal source: periods, pregnancy, an operation or a medical worry can all bring it into dreams. In those cases the dream is usually simply processing what the body is going through.

How common is it?

Blood in dreams usually comes with violence or injury, which are common themes: in a survey of 1,181 Canadian students, 42% had dreamed of being physically attacked and 35% of being killed.

Source: Nielsen et al. (2003), “The Typical Dreams of Canadian University Students”, Dreaming 13(4): 211–235. 1,181 first-year students were asked whether they had ever had each of 55 dream themes.

Frequently asked questions

Is dreaming of blood a bad sign?

Most old books said so, with one exception: Fontaine saw money and children in it. Today it is usually read as a sign of stress, hurt or fatigue rather than a prediction.

What does it mean to dream of blood on your hands?

It is mostly linked to guilt or responsibility: a feeling that you caused harm or made a mistake, even a small one.

What does it mean to dream of bleeding?

It often reflects feeling drained, physically or emotionally. If you have a health worry, the dream may simply be echoing it.
