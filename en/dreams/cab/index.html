Dreaming of a Cab

cab, hackney, taxi · ●●●●● mixed

A cab points to a busy life and modest prosperity, but also to secrets and the risk of gossip.

What happens in the dream

  • Riding in a cab: pleasant occupations and moderate prosperity
  • Driving a public cab: manual work with little room to advance
  • Riding in a cab at night with others: a secret you will try to keep from friends
  • Riding in a cab with someone of bad repute: gossip will link your name with theirs

How you felt

  • Being carried along: the dream often mirrors a sense of following a route someone else set
  • Awkwardness: it may reflect worry that the company you keep looks bad

Ask yourself

  • Am I steering my life right now, or being driven?
  • Is there something I am keeping from people close to me?

See also Carriage · Coach (Horse-Drawn) · Ride · Secret · Coach Driver

Source: G. H. Miller, Ten Thousand Dreams Interpreted, or What's in a Dream (1901). Also searched as: hackney, taxi.
