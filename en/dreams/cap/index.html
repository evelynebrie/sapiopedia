Dreaming of a Cap

cap, beret · ●●●●● mixed

A cap can mean a party, shyness, marriage or an inheritance, but also secrets coming out.

What happens in the dream

  • A miner's cap: a comfortable inheritance
  • Putting on a cap: be careful in love
  • Seeing a cap: an invitation to some festivity
  • Your sweetheart wearing a cap: you will feel shy around them
  • Taking a cap off: what you want to hide will come out
  • Being given a cap: you will soon marry
  • A prisoner's cap: your courage is failing in a moment of danger

How you felt

  • Identity: the dream often mirrors the role you are taking on
  • Self-consciousness: it may reflect fear of being found out

Ask yourself

  • What role am I playing right now?
  • Is there something I'm afraid will be discovered?

See also Hat · Bonnet · Prisoners · Wedding · Inheritance

Source: G. H. Miller, Ten Thousand Dreams Interpreted, or What's in a Dream (1901); F. Fontaine, The Golden Wheel Dream-Book and Fortune-Teller (1862). Also searched as: beret.
