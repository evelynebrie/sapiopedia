Dreaming of Chrysanthemums

chrysanthemum, aster, mum · ●●●●● mostly negative, with some happy notes

White chrysanthemums speak of loss and sadness; coloured ones of pleasant meetings.

What happens in the dream

  • Coloured chrysanthemums: pleasant engagements
  • Chrysanthemums in bouquets: love will be offered, but a misguided ambition will make you set it aside
  • Feeling your spirit leave your body among the white flowers: a crisis is coming soon
  • Picking white chrysanthemums: loss and much bewilderment
  • Walking down an avenue of white chrysanthemums with a few yellow ones: a strange sense of loss and sadness, from which you'll come out more sensitive and stronger

How you felt

  • Grief or nostalgia: the dream may reflect a loss you haven't finished accepting
  • A kind of uplift: sadness can also open you to a new sensitivity

Ask yourself

  • What loss do I still need to work through?
  • Have I pushed love aside for the sake of ambition?

See also Bouquet · Rose · Lily · Funeral · Daisy

Source: G. H. Miller, Ten Thousand Dreams Interpreted, or What's in a Dream (1901). Also searched as: aster, mum.
