Dreaming of a Crown

crown, tiara · ●●●●● mixed

A crown foretells change and elevation, but also losses depending on how it appears.

What happens in the dream

  • A gold crown is placed on your head: success and great honour
  • A silver crown: good health
  • Another reading: success and rise to high office; if you are single, marriage to someone hard-working or wealthy
  • You crown someone: your own worthiness
  • A crown of green leaves: friends and fortune will desert you
  • You see a crown: a change in your way of life, travel far from home and new relationships; sometimes serious illness
  • You wear a crown: loss of personal property

How you felt

  • A need for recognition: the dream often reflects a wish to be acknowledged
  • The weight of responsibility: you may be carrying a heavy load

Ask yourself

  • What recognition am I seeking?
  • Are my responsibilities weighing on me?
