Dreaming of a Curb

curb, kerb, sidewalk · ●●●●● positive, unless you fall

Stepping up onto a curb foretells a quick rise; falling off one, a reversal of fortune.

What happens in the dream

  • You step onto a curb: a quick rise in business and the esteem of friends and the public
  • Lovers step onto a curb together: an early marriage and lasting fidelity
  • You step or fall off a curb: your fortunes will be reversed

How you felt

  • Momentum: the dream may reflect a sense of moving up a step
  • Caution: you may fear a misstep

Ask yourself

  • What step am I taking up?
  • Where do I need to watch my step?

See also Street · Stumble · Fall · Steps · Promenade

Source: G. H. Miller, Ten Thousand Dreams Interpreted, or What's in a Dream (1901). Also searched as: kerb, sidewalk.
