Dreaming of Death

death · ●●●●● mixed: often the opposite of what it shows

“To die, to sleep;
To sleep, perchance to dream”

William Shakespeare, Hamlet, c. 1600

Dreaming of death is frightening but very common, and it rarely announces a real death. The old dream books often read it the other way round, as news of a wedding or a long life; today it is mostly understood as a dream about endings and change.

Summary: Death in a dream sometimes brings sadness and bad news, but often a wedding, long life or loved ones in good health.

What happens in the dream

  • You see death itself: happiness and long life, a coming wedding, yours or someone else's
  • You are dead yourself: a speedy marriage and success in your undertakings
  • Your living friends appear dead: a sign they are in good health
  • You see someone else dead: quick news from friends, or unkind treatment from them
  • You see one of your family dead: sorrow and disappointment
  • You hear of a friend or relative's death: bad news from them soon

How you felt

  • Fear: the dream often reflects anxiety about losing someone
  • Transformation: part of your life may be ending to make room for something new

Ask yourself

  • What is ending for me?
  • What could be born from this ending?

What the old dream books said

F. Fontaine1862

Seeing death itself means happiness and long life, and that you will soon marry or attend a wedding. Seeing another person dead means hurried news from friends.

A. H. Noe1885

Dreaming of death, or that you are dead, means a speedy marriage and success. Seeing someone else dead means unkind treatment from your friends.

G. H. Miller1901

Darker: seeing your family dead warns of sorrow and disappointment, and hearing of a friend’s death brings bad news. But Miller adds that death dreams are often misleading and may only mean that one thought or habit is replacing another.

What it often means today

Modern readings agree with Miller’s second thought more than with his warnings. Death in a dream usually stands for an ending: a job, a relationship, a stage of life, an old idea of yourself. Seeing yourself die is often linked to a period of transformation, sometimes one you welcome.

Freud devoted a well-known section of The Interpretation of Dreams (1900) to dreams of the death of dear relatives. He argued that, despite their painful content, they connect to old childhood feelings, rivalries and wishes long forgotten, rather than to any present wish. Few people follow his reading in full today, but he was right that these dreams say more about the dreamer than about the person who dies in them.

Dreaming that someone living has died is most often a sign of worry for them, or of a change in the relationship: a child leaving home, a friend drifting away. If the dream follows a real loss, it is part of grieving and tends to fade with time.

How common is it?

It is one of the typical dreams. In a survey of 1,181 Canadian students, 54% had dreamed that someone still alive was dead, and 24% had seen themselves dead.

Source: Nielsen et al. (2003), “The Typical Dreams of Canadian University Students”, Dreaming 13(4): 211–235. 1,181 first-year students were asked whether they had ever had each of 55 dream themes.

Frequently asked questions

Does dreaming of death mean someone will die?

No. Nothing shows that these dreams predict deaths. More than half of the students in a large survey had dreamed that a living person was dead, which would be impossible if the dream were a warning.

What does it mean to dream of my own death?

It is usually read as a symbol of change: something in your life is ending to make room for something new. The old books even saw it as a sign of marriage and success.

Why do I dream of people who have died?

It is a normal part of grief. Such dreams are frequent after a loss and are often calm or comforting; they become rarer as time passes.

What does it mean to dream of death?

Usually an ending or a change in your life, not a real death. The old books even read it as a wedding or a long life.

Is it normal to dream of death?

Yes, very. More than half of the students in a large survey had dreamed that someone living was dead.

What does death symbolize in a dream?

Endings, transformation and the close of a chapter.
