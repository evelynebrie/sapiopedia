Dreaming of a Duet

duet, duo, harmony, pair · ●●●●● mixed

An instrumental duet means a peaceful life for lovers; a sung duet, slightly sad news soon forgotten.

What happens in the dream

  • You hear a duet sung: unpleasant news from someone far away, soon displaced by a new pleasure
  • You sing a duet with someone you're drawn to: you can win them if you wish
  • You hear a duet played: a calm, quarrel-free life for lovers, mild rivalry in business
  • If you're a musician: competition and wrangling for first place
  • You sing with someone else: a secret but powerful enemy

How you felt

  • Harmony: the dream often reflects how in tune you feel with someone
  • Rivalry: perhaps a sense of having to share the stage

Ask yourself

  • Who am I truly in tune with?
  • Do I treat a relationship as a partnership or a competition?

See also Singing · Music · Concert · Quartet

Source: G. H. Miller, Ten Thousand Dreams Interpreted, or What's in a Dream (1901); F. Fontaine, The Golden Wheel Dream-Book and Fortune-Teller (1862). Also searched as: duo, harmony, pair.
