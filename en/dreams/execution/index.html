Dreaming of an Execution

execution, death penalty, hanging · ●●●●● mixed

An execution in a dream is often read by contraries: being condemned yourself means luck, seeing someone else executed calls for care.

What happens in the dream

  • You're about to be executed and are saved at the last moment: you'll defeat your enemies and grow rich
  • You're about to be hanged: a lucky year and success in business
  • You see someone hanged: a friend or relative will soon come into good fortune
  • Offenders are executed: people in great need will suddenly turn to you for help
  • You witness an execution: misfortune through other people's carelessness

How you felt

  • Fear of judgment: the dream often reflects guilt or pressure
  • Relief: the dream's happy outcome may point to an ordeal you'll get through

Ask yourself

  • Who or what makes me feel judged?
  • What ordeal am I going through right now?

Source: G. H. Miller, Ten Thousand Dreams Interpreted, or What's in a Dream (1901); F. Fontaine, The Golden Wheel Dream-Book and Fortune-Teller (1862); A. H. Noe, The Witches' Dream Book (1885). Also searched as: death penalty, hanging.
