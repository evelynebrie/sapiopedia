Dreaming of a Fall

fall, plunge, trip · ●●●●● mixed, depending on the fall

“Him the Almighty Power
Hurled headlong flaming from th’ ethereal sky”

John Milton, Paradise Lost, 1667

Dreaming of falling is the most common of all typical dreams after being chased. The old dream books disagreed about it, some seeing a rise in fortune, others a loss. Today it is usually linked to insecurity, loss of control, or simply the moment of falling asleep.

Summary: A fall in a dream can mean a struggle followed by a rise, or a loss if you get hurt.

What happens in the dream

  • You fall and are badly frightened but unhurt: a great struggle, then honor and wealth
  • You fall from a height: in one reading, you'll be given a position of dignity or marry well
  • You fall from a high place or a tree: in another reading, loss of position and goods, and in love, a match that won't happen
  • You're injured in the fall: hardship and loss of friends

How you felt

  • Fear of losing your footing: the dream often reflects a loss of control
  • Insecurity: you may fear you can't hold your position

Ask yourself

  • Where do I feel the ground slipping away?
  • What would help me feel more stable?

What the old dream books said

G. H. Miller1901

A frightening fall means a great struggle that will end in honour and wealth; but if you are hurt in the fall, hardship and the loss of friends.

F. Fontaine1862

Falling from a height means a dignity will be conferred on you, or that you will marry above your station; for a young woman, a rich husband.

A. H. Noe1885

The opposite: falling from a high place or a tree means losing your position and goods, and for a lover, never marrying the person you love.

What it often means today

Falling is usually read as a loss of footing: a job that feels uncertain, a relationship that is wobbling, a sense that things are moving too fast. The dream gives a physical shape to the fear of losing control.

Some falling dreams have a simpler cause. The sudden jolt many people feel just as they fall asleep, the “hypnic jerk”, often comes with a brief image of tripping or falling. Freud already reviewed explanations of this kind, from writers who linked falling dreams to the body’s sensations at the edge of sleep, before arguing that the sensations of falling are borrowed from memory to express the dream’s own thoughts.

Where you fall and how it ends matter. Landing safely, or waking before the impact, often reflects a fear that turns out to be manageable. Being pushed suggests that you feel someone else is undermining you. Falling and then flying turns the dream around: from losing control to finding your feet.

How common is it?

It is one of the most widespread dreams of all: in a survey of 1,181 Canadian students, 74% had dreamed of falling and 58% of being on the verge of falling.

Source: Nielsen et al. (2003), “The Typical Dreams of Canadian University Students”, Dreaming 13(4): 211–235. 1,181 first-year students were asked whether they had ever had each of 55 dream themes.

Frequently asked questions

Is it true that you die if you hit the ground in a falling dream?

No. It is a popular myth. Many people dream of landing and wake up perfectly well.

Why do I jerk awake when I dream of falling?

That is usually a hypnic jerk, a sudden muscle twitch as you fall asleep. The brain often wraps it in a short image of tripping or falling.

What does it mean to dream of falling from a height?

It is usually read as fear of losing your position or control. Fontaine, oddly, saw it as a promotion or a good marriage.

What does it mean to dream of falling?

Usually insecurity or loss of control, and sometimes just the jolt of falling asleep.

Is it normal to dream of falling?

Yes. It is one of the most common dreams of all: three people in four have had it.

What does falling symbolize in a dream?

Loss of footing, fear of failure, and letting go.
