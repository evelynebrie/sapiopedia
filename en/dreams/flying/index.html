Dreaming of Flying

flying, gliding, levitating, soaring · ●●●●● mostly positive, depending on the flight

“Higher still and higher
From the earth thou springest”

Percy Bysshe Shelley, “To a Skylark”, 1820

Dreaming that you can fly is one of the happiest typical dreams. Most of the old dream books saw it as an excellent omen, and today it is usually linked to freedom, confidence and the feeling of rising above a situation.

Summary: Flying is often an excellent omen of rising fortune and happiness, but it depends how high you go, what lies below and whether you fall.

What happens in the dream

  • You are flying: a rise in fortune, lasting happiness and a faithful partner
  • You fall while flying: a downfall; if you wake as you fall, you will recover
  • You fly through the air: a quick journey or sudden news
  • You fly very high: trouble in your marriage or relationship
  • You fly close to the ground: illness or unease you will recover from
  • You fly over muddy water or broken ground: bad luck; guard your private affairs
  • You fly over green trees or see the sun: prosperity despite your fears
  • You try to fly and cannot: some restraint will hold you back from your goal
  • You fly on black wings, or out past the planets: bitter disappointment and troubles

How you felt

  • Freedom: the dream often reflects drive and a wish to rise
  • Anxiety: a difficult flight may show a fear of losing your footing

Ask yourself

  • What would I like to break free from?
  • What keeps me from taking off?

What the old dream books said

G. H. Miller1901

The most detailed and the most cautious: flying high means marital trouble, flying low means illness you will recover from, flying over green fields means prosperity after a setback. Falling while flying means your downfall.

A. H. Noe1885

A very excellent omen: rising fortune, honours, a faithful sweetheart, many happy children and an advantageous journey. Fontaine says almost word for word the same.

The Interpretation of Dreams and Moles1805

Flying through the air means a quick journey or hasty news. Trying to fly and failing means a restraint on what you are pursuing.

What it often means today

Flying is usually read as freedom: escaping a constraint, gaining perspective, feeling capable. How you fly matters. Gliding easily suggests confidence; struggling to stay up, or flying too low, points to effort and doubt, close to the old idea that failing to fly means being held back.

Freud noted that earlier writers had explained flying dreams by the movement of the lungs during sleep. He argued instead that the sensations of motion in these dreams are taken from memory and reused to express what the dream is about. Whatever their source, these dreams are remembered for the pleasure they give.

Flying dreams are also closely tied to lucid dreaming: many people who realise they are dreaming choose to fly. If you often fly in dreams, you may be close to becoming aware that you are dreaming.

How common is it?

It is one of the most common typical dreams: in a survey of 1,181 Canadian students, 48% had dreamed of flying or soaring through the air, men a little more often than women.

Source: Nielsen et al. (2003), “The Typical Dreams of Canadian University Students”, Dreaming 13(4): 211–235. 1,181 first-year students were asked whether they had ever had each of 55 dream themes.

Frequently asked questions

Is dreaming of flying a good sign?

Most old dream books said so, and modern readings agree that it usually reflects freedom or confidence. Miller was the exception, with many warnings depending on how you fly.

Why can’t I fly high in my dreams?

Struggling to take off or to stay up often reflects effort, doubt or something holding you back. The 1805 dream book read it the same way: a restraint on what you are pursuing.

Is flying in a dream a sign of lucid dreaming?

It often goes with it. Many lucid dreamers fly once they realise they are dreaming, and frequent flying dreams can be a step towards lucidity.

What does it mean to dream of flying?

Usually freedom, confidence or rising above a problem. Struggling to fly points to effort or doubt.

Is it normal to dream of flying?

Yes, very. Almost half of the students in a large survey had dreamed of flying.

What does flying symbolize in a dream?

Freedom, perspective and ambition.
