Dreaming of a Ghost

ghost · ●●●●● mostly negative, with some bright spots

“All houses wherein men have lived and died
Are haunted houses.”

Henry Wadsworth Longfellow, “Haunted Houses”, 1858

Dreaming of a ghost usually feels eerie, and the old dream books mostly saw danger, false friends or loss in it. Today a ghost is often read as something from the past that has not been settled: a person, a memory or a feeling that still comes back.

Summary: A ghost mostly warns of danger, false friends and bereavement, with a few brighter visions.

What happens in the dream

  • A ghost speaks to you: someone is trying to lure you into a trap; a grave omen in the old books
  • A calm figure floating through the sky: progress in study and almost miraculous wealth, with an undertone of sadness
  • Simply seeing a ghost: possibly a wedding in the family, the happiest reading here
  • The ghost of a parent: danger; be wary of going into partnership with strangers
  • The ghost of a friend who has died: a long trip with unpleasant company, and disappointments
  • The ghost of someone still living: a friend may bear you ill will; keep your affairs under your own eye, especially if the ghost looks haggard
  • A ghost or an angel in the sky: losses in the family

How you felt

  • Fear or unease: the dream often reflects something from the past that isn't settled
  • Longing: you may be thinking of someone who has died or is far away

Ask yourself

  • What from my past keeps coming back?
  • Is there someone I haven't finished grieving?

What the old dream books said

G. H. Miller1901

The ghost of a parent warns of danger and of risky partnerships; the ghost of a dead friend, an unpleasant journey. A ghost that speaks to you means falling into enemies’ hands. A calm figure floating in the sky brings progress and wealth, with a note of sadness.

F. Fontaine1862

An apparition dressed in white means deceit, temptation and disappointment in love, summed up in an old verse: your sweetheart has a fair face but a devil’s heart.

A. H. Noe1885

Contradictory: seeing a ghost means a marriage in the family, but if it speaks, prepare to die. Elsewhere Noe says that spectres bring work to the artist and marriage to the maiden.

Read these as tradition, not prediction.

What it often means today

A ghost is something that should be gone but is not. That is why modern readings connect ghost dreams to unfinished business: a grief, a guilt, a relationship that ended badly, or simply an old fear. The ghost of someone you know often reflects your feelings about them more than anything about them.

Freud offered a very down-to-earth explanation for the fear of ghosts in dreams. In The Interpretation of Dreams he wrote that robbers and ghosts, the nightly visitors we fear before sleep, go back to childhood memories of adults coming to the bedside at night, “the ghosts more probably” being women in white nightgowns. Whatever one thinks of his reading, it is a reminder that night fears often start very early.

Some ghost dreams are not frightening at all. A calm, silent presence, or a loved one who has died appearing at peace, is usually read as part of remembering and saying goodbye.

How common is it?

Feeling a presence is a typical dream experience: in a survey of 1,181 Canadian students, 48% had dreamed of vividly sensing a presence in the room, and 38% had dreamed of someone who has died as if they were alive.

Source: Nielsen et al. (2003), “The Typical Dreams of Canadian University Students”, Dreaming 13(4): 211–235. 1,181 first-year students were asked whether they had ever had each of 55 dream themes.

Frequently asked questions

Is dreaming of a ghost a sign from the dead?

There is no evidence for it. These dreams are common, especially after a loss or during stressful periods, and they are best read as a way of processing memories and feelings.

What does it mean when a ghost talks to you in a dream?

The old books saw it as a warning. Today it is usually read as a message from yourself: something you need to hear or acknowledge.

Why do I dream of ghosts when I am stressed?

Stress makes dreams more vivid and more threatening. A ghost gives a shape to an unease that has no clear source.
