Dreaming of Ironing

ironing, pressing · ●●●●● mixed, leaning favourable

Ironing points to home comforts and orderly affairs, unless something goes wrong with the job.

What happens in the dream

  • Ironing: home comforts and well-run affairs
  • Burning your hands while ironing: illness or jealousy will disturb your peace
  • The iron stays cold: a lack of affection at home
  • Scorching the clothes: a rival will stir up suspicion and displeasure

How you felt

  • A need for order: the dream often reflects a wish to smooth out a situation
  • Household load: the weight of everyday chores

Ask yourself

  • What would I like to smooth out?
  • Do I get as much warmth at home as I give?
