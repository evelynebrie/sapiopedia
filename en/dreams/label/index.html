Dreaming of a Label

label, sticker, tag, ticket · ●●●●● mostly negative

A label warns against carelessness that could expose your affairs to someone who means you harm.

What happens in the dream

  • A label: through carelessness you'll let an enemy see into your private affairs, and you'll pay for it

How you felt

  • Vulnerability: the dream may reflect fear of being exposed or judged
  • Feeling pigeonholed: you may feel reduced to a label

Ask yourself

  • Am I sharing too much with certain people?
  • What label do others put on me, or do I put on myself?

See also Secret · Privacy · Envelope · Enemy · Letter

Source: G. H. Miller, Ten Thousand Dreams Interpreted, or What's in a Dream (1901). Also searched as: sticker, tag, ticket.
