Dreaming of Lightning

lightning, lightning bolt, thunderbolt, thunderstorm · ●●●●● mixed

Lightning brings sharp but brief happiness: its color, direction and nearness tell whether it means luck or trial.

What happens in the dream

  • Lightning: happiness and prosperity, but short-lived
  • Bright white or bluish-silver lightning: a pleasant trip, good crops and thriving business
  • Lightning above your head: joy and gain
  • Livid lightning splitting black clouds: sorrow will follow close behind good fortune
  • Lightning strikes you: unexpected grief in love or business
  • Lightning in the east, southwest or west: luck coming your way; in the north or south: obstacles, or luck that hides for a while
  • Lightning from dark, ominous clouds: losses and disappointments; keep your loved ones close
  • Lightning striking near you without touching you: a change of place, or gossip

How you felt

  • Surprise or excitement: the dream may reflect a sudden insight
  • Fear: it may express a shock or upheaval you dread

Ask yourself

  • What has struck me like lightning lately?
  • Am I wary of happiness that comes too fast?
