Dreaming of Nails

nails, nail · ●●●●● mixed

Iron nails mean hard work for little pay; long fingernails, prosperity; cut or torn nails, loss.

What happens in the dream

  • You see nails: much toil and small reward
  • You deal in nails: honourable work, however humble
  • Your fingernails are longer than usual: profit, prosperity, success in love and a welcome sum of money
  • Rusty or broken nails: illness and business failure
  • Your nails are cut off: loss, disgrace and quarrels with relatives
  • Your nails are pulled out: misery and serious hardship
  • You bite your nails: quarrels and discord
  • You pare your nails: a lingering illness

How you felt

  • Tension: the dream often reflects nervousness or sustained effort
  • Self-care: it may show how much attention you pay to your appearance or work

Ask yourself

  • What is making me nervous right now?
  • Is my work valued for what it's worth?
