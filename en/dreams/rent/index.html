Dreaming of Rent

rent, landlord, lease · ●●●●● mixed

Renting or paying rent in a dream points to good business; being unable to pay, to losses.

What happens in the dream

  • You rent a house: new, profitable contracts
  • You fail to rent out property: a slack spell in business
  • You pay your rent: your finances will be satisfactory, perhaps with some unexpected money
  • You can't pay and worry about it: falling trade and loss of property

How you felt

  • Money worries: the dream often reflects a concern for security
  • A need for stability: perhaps a wish to feel at home

Ask yourself

  • What makes me feel financially secure?
  • Where do I truly feel at home?

See also House · Money · Debt · Tenant

Source: G. H. Miller, Ten Thousand Dreams Interpreted, or What's in a Dream (1901); F. Fontaine, The Golden Wheel Dream-Book and Fortune-Teller (1862). Also searched as: landlord, lease.
