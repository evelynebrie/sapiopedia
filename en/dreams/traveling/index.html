Dreaming of Traveling

traveling, travel, trip · ●●●●● mostly positive, depending on the road

Traveling blends profit and pleasure; it all depends on the landscape you cross.

What happens in the dream

  • You travel: profit and pleasure combined
  • Through green, fertile hills: great prosperity and happiness
  • In a crowded carriage: lucky adventures and entertaining new companions
  • By train or boat: thriving business; in your own private coach: passing luck that ends badly
  • You cross the sea and land abroad: luck in all your dealings
  • Over bare, rocky slopes: apparent gain followed by loss, or success won the hard way
  • Through rough, unknown country: dangerous enemies, perhaps illness
  • You travel alone: an eventful trip, but worrying affairs
  • You get caught in briars in a wood: many setbacks

How you felt

  • Momentum: a wish for novelty and movement
  • Uncertainty: fear of not knowing where you're headed

Ask yourself

  • What do I want to move towards?
  • Does the road I'm on suit me?

Source: G. H. Miller, Ten Thousand Dreams Interpreted, or What's in a Dream (1901); F. Fontaine, The Golden Wheel Dream-Book and Fortune-Teller (1862); A. H. Noe, The Witches' Dream Book (1885). Also searched as: travel, trip.
