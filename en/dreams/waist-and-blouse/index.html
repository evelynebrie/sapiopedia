Dreaming of a Waist and Blouse

waist and blouse, blouse, waist, waistline · ●●●●● mostly positive

A full waist and a pretty blouse promise favor and admiration; a torn blouse brings criticism.

What happens in the dream

  • A full, rounded waist: a pleasant turn of good fortune
  • A nice ready-made blouse: your ingenuity and charm will win admiration
  • A small, unnatural waist: disappointing success and bickering
  • A torn blouse: you will be criticized over an affair
  • Trying on a blouse: a rival in love, whom you will beat if the blouse fits

How you felt

  • Concern about appearance: the dream may reflect how you see your body
  • A wish to please: you may wonder whether you measure up to a rival

Ask yourself

  • How do I feel in my body right now?
  • Who do I want to impress, and why?

See also Shirt · Corset · Girdle · Clothes · Dressing

Source: G. H. Miller, Ten Thousand Dreams Interpreted, or What's in a Dream (1901). Also searched as: blouse, waist, waistline.
