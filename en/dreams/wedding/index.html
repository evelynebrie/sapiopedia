Dreaming of a Wedding

wedding, ceremony · ●●●●● mostly negative, with some happy exceptions

“Let me not to the marriage of true minds
Admit impediments.”

William Shakespeare, Sonnet 116, 1609

Dreaming of a wedding sounds happy, but the old dream books were famously suspicious of it: for them a merry wedding often meant a funeral. Today wedding dreams are usually read as dreams about commitment, a decision or a new stage of life, whether or not you are planning to marry.

Summary: Traditionally, a wedding in a dream is a paradox: it points to bitterness or bereavement more often than joy.

What happens in the dream

  • Attending a wedding: a bitter occasion and delayed success
  • Making a match everyone approves of: you will rise in others' esteem and your hopes will come true
  • Marrying someone handsome: joy; someone unattractive: discontent
  • A merry wedding: by tradition, a sign of a funeral
  • Marrying in secret: a bad sign for your reputation
  • Your family objects: your engagement will upset your relatives
  • Your partner marries someone else: needless fears, as they will keep their promises
  • Kissing the bride, or being kissed by her: the loss of someone dear
  • Someone in mourning clothes at your wedding: an unhappy married life

How you felt

  • Excitement or nerves: the dream often reflects an approaching commitment or big change
  • Worry: you may be wondering what you're leaving behind

Ask yourself

  • What commitment am I making?
  • What is changing in my life right now?

What the old dream books said

G. H. Miller1901

Attending a wedding brings bitterness and delays. A marriage everyone approves of brings respect; a secret one, scandal. Seeing a marriage with guests in bright colours means great enjoyment; guests in black mean mourning.

F. Fontaine1862

Being at a jolly wedding is a sign of a funeral, and kissing the bride foretells the death of a friend or relative. Dreaming that you marry announces your own death.

A. H. Noe1885

Dreaming that you are married is unfavourable, a sign of poverty and misfortune. But attending a wedding is a forerunner of pleasing news and great success.

What it often means today

Today a wedding in a dream is usually read as a symbol of union and commitment rather than a forecast. It can be about a real relationship, but just as often about a new job, a move or a promise you have made to yourself. Marrying someone unexpected, a colleague or a stranger, often points to a quality you want to bring into your life rather than to that person.

When the wedding goes wrong, the dream is usually about anxiety. The dress does not fit, you arrive late, the guests do not come: these are the classic stress dreams before a big event, and they are well known among people who are actually engaged. They say more about the pressure to get things right than about the relationship itself.

Watching someone else marry, especially an ex, often stirs up questions about your own choices and the paths you did not take. A calm, joyful ceremony, on the other hand, is generally read as harmony between parts of your life that had been pulling apart.

How common is it?

No study has counted wedding dreams as such, but the anxieties they usually stage are among the most common dream themes: in a survey of 1,181 Canadian students, 60% had dreamed of arriving too late and 33% of being inappropriately dressed.

Source: Nielsen et al. (2003), “The Typical Dreams of Canadian University Students”, Dreaming 13(4): 211–235. 1,181 first-year students were asked whether they had ever had each of 55 dream themes.

Frequently asked questions

Is dreaming of a wedding a bad omen?

The old books often said so, linking weddings to funerals. Nothing supports that today; a wedding dream is usually about commitment or change.

What does it mean to dream of my ex getting married?

It often brings up feelings about the past or about your own path, rather than any wish to get back together. It can also mark the end of a chapter.

Why do I dream of my wedding going wrong?

If your wedding is coming up, it is a very common stress dream. Otherwise it may reflect fear of failing at an important commitment or of being judged.

What does it mean to dream of a wedding?

Usually commitment, a decision or a new stage of life. Stressful wedding dreams reflect pressure to get things right.

Is it normal to dream of a wedding?

Yes, especially before a real wedding or any big commitment. Running late and wrong clothes are classic stress themes.

What does a wedding symbolize in a dream?

Union, commitment and new beginnings; in the old books, oddly, often a funeral.
