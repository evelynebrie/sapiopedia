Dreaming of Whisky

whisky, bourbon, scotch · ●●●●● mostly negative

Whisky mostly signals disappointment, though it can reward persistence.

What happens in the dream

  • Whisky in bottles: you will guard your interests closely and grow them
  • Drinking it alone: you will sacrifice friends to selfishness
  • Destroying it: stinginess will cost you friends
  • Drinking it: you will reach your goal after many letdowns
  • Only seeing it: you won't get the result you hoped for

How you felt

  • A need for comfort: the dream may reflect a wish to unwind
  • Loneliness: you may be withdrawing into yourself a bit too much

Ask yourself

  • How am I unwinding these days?
  • Have I made time to share things with friends?

See also Brandy · Liquor · Drinking · Rum · Gin

Source: G. H. Miller, Ten Thousand Dreams Interpreted, or What's in a Dream (1901). Also searched as: bourbon, scotch.
