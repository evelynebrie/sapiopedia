60 icebreaker questions for groups: work, family and new friends

60 original icebreaker questions for team meetings, family dinners and first evenings with new friends. Random draw and full list.

A good icebreaker is easy to answer, impossible to answer badly, and leaves a small door open for a story. These 60 questions were written for three settings where silence feels loudest: the start of a meeting, a long family dinner, and that first evening with people you barely know. Pick a setting and draw.

Press the button to draw a question.

At work

  1. What is the best piece of advice you received in your first job?
  2. If your job had a theme song, what would it be?
  3. What is a small thing that makes your workday better?
  4. Which skill would you love to learn if you had a free afternoon every week?
  5. What was your very first job, and what did it teach you?
  6. What is one tool or app you could not work without?
  7. Coffee, tea, or something else to start the day?
  8. What is the most interesting thing you have learned this month?
  9. If you could swap jobs with anyone here for a day, who would it be?
  10. What is your favourite way to take a break?
  11. What is a work habit you are quietly proud of?
  12. Which meeting would you replace with an email, if you could choose just one?
  13. What is something you were nervous about that turned out fine?
  14. What is a book, podcast or show that changed how you think about work?
  15. What do you do in the first ten minutes of your day?
  16. What is the kindest thing a colleague has done for you?
  17. If you could add one room to the office, what would it be?
  18. What is your go-to lunch when you have five minutes?
  19. What do people often misunderstand about your job?
  20. What would you tell yourself on your first day here?

Family dinner

  1. What is a family tradition you would like to keep forever?
  2. What was the best meal anyone at this table ever cooked?
  3. What is a story about a grandparent that everyone should hear?
  4. Which family holiday do you remember most vividly, and why?
  5. What did you want to be when you were little?
  6. What is something you learned from someone at this table?
  7. What is the most useful thing you own that cost almost nothing?
  8. If our family had a motto, what would it be?
  9. What was your favourite toy or game growing up?
  10. What is a smell that instantly takes you back to childhood?
  11. Who in the family tells the best jokes, and who tells the longest ones?
  12. What is one thing you would like to learn from an older relative?
  13. What is a dish you only eat at family gatherings?
  14. What is the funniest misunderstanding that ever happened in this family?
  15. Which photo in the family album do you like best?
  16. What is the best advice a parent or grandparent gave you?
  17. If we could all travel somewhere together, where would you pick?
  18. What is a habit you picked up from your parents without noticing?
  19. What is the first thing you remember cooking?
  20. What is something you are looking forward to this year?

New friends

  1. What is a hobby you picked up as an adult?
  2. What is the best thing you have eaten this month?
  3. Which place in the world do you think is underrated?
  4. What is a song you never skip?
  5. What is something you are weirdly good at?
  6. What was the last thing that made you laugh out loud?
  7. Early bird or night owl, and has it always been that way?
  8. What is a show or book you would recommend to anyone?
  9. What is a small ritual that makes your weekend feel like a weekend?
  10. If you could have dinner with any living person, who would it be?
  11. What is the most unexpected compliment you have received?
  12. What did you learn recently that surprised you?
  13. What is your ideal Sunday afternoon?
  14. What is one thing on your bucket list that is actually realistic?
  15. Which childhood food do you still love unapologetically?
  16. What is your favourite word in any language?
  17. If you could instantly master one instrument, which would it be?
  18. What is a place in this city you think more people should know about?
  19. What is the strangest fact you know by heart?
  20. What is one thing you are really proud of that few people know about?

Rules

  • Choose the setting that matches your group and draw one question.
  • Go around the table: everyone answers the same question, 30 seconds each.
  • Follow-up questions are welcome, but only after everyone has answered once.
  • Nobody is forced to answer. "Pass" is a complete sentence; draw another one.
  • For large groups, split into tables of four to six so everyone gets to speak.
  • Three questions is usually plenty: stop while the energy is still high.
