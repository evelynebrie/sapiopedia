Never Have I Ever

50 clean never-have-I-ever prompts about travel, food, everyday life and small confessions. Random draw plus the full list.

"Never have I ever" is a confession game disguised as a counting game: someone reads a statement, and everyone who has done it puts a finger down. The best prompts are the ones half the room admits to with a groan. These 50 are clean, playable at a family table, and need nothing but ten fingers.

Press the button to draw a question.

Travel

  1. Never have I ever missed a flight.
  2. Never have I ever slept in an airport.
  3. Never have I ever gotten on the wrong train and stayed on it anyway.
  4. Never have I ever pretended to understand a local when I understood nothing.
  5. Never have I ever packed an outfit I did not wear once during the whole trip.
  6. Never have I ever photographed a monument without actually looking at it.
  7. Never have I ever sent a postcard that arrived after I got home.
  8. Never have I ever left a hotel with the key card still in my pocket.
  9. Never have I ever spent half a trip looking for Wi-Fi.
  10. Never have I ever ordered the one thing on the menu I could pronounce.
  11. Never have I ever walked into a shop just to ask for directions, then bought something out of guilt.
  12. Never have I ever pretended to have visited a place everyone else had been to.

Food

  1. Never have I ever eaten cereal for dinner three nights in a row.
  2. Never have I ever drunk juice straight from the carton.
  3. Never have I ever finished someone else's plate in a restaurant.
  4. Never have I ever passed off a shop-bought dish as homemade.
  5. Never have I ever eaten something off the floor and invoked the five-second rule.
  6. Never have I ever burned a dish so badly I had to open every window.
  7. Never have I ever put ketchup on something that really did not need it.
  8. Never have I ever hidden a snack so I would not have to share it.
  9. Never have I ever eaten the garnish because I thought it was part of the meal.
  10. Never have I ever gone to a restaurant alone and loved it.
  11. Never have I ever ordered delivery from a place I could see from my window.
  12. Never have I ever said "I'm not that hungry" and then eaten half of everyone's fries.

Everyday life

  1. Never have I ever waved back at someone who was waving at the person behind me.
  2. Never have I ever said "you too" to a waiter who told me to enjoy my meal.
  3. Never have I ever walked into a room and completely forgotten why.
  4. Never have I ever pretended to be on the phone to avoid a conversation.
  5. Never have I ever laughed at a joke I did not understand.
  6. Never have I ever worn the same outfit two days in a row and hoped nobody noticed.
  7. Never have I ever replied "sorry, just saw this" to a message I read hours ago.
  8. Never have I ever held a door for someone so far away they had to jog.
  9. Never have I ever pulled a push door in front of a crowd.
  10. Never have I ever talked to my pet as if it were going to answer.
  11. Never have I ever rehearsed a conversation in the shower.
  12. Never have I ever secretly googled a word mid-conversation so I would not look clueless.
  13. Never have I ever spent more time choosing a show than actually watching it.

Confessions

  1. Never have I ever pretended to love a gift and then donated it.
  2. Never have I ever blamed the dog, the cat or a sibling for something I did.
  3. Never have I ever read the last page of a book first.
  4. Never have I ever faked a yawn to end a phone call.
  5. Never have I ever secretly re-sorted a dishwasher someone else had loaded.
  6. Never have I ever said "I'm on my way" while still sitting on the sofa.
  7. Never have I ever accidentally liked a very old post while scrolling through someone's profile.
  8. Never have I ever invented a plan to get out of another plan.
  9. Never have I ever forgotten a birthday and then sent a message pretending I had planned to call.
  10. Never have I ever kept a group chat on mute for over a year.
  11. Never have I ever cried at an advert.
  12. Never have I ever taken credit for a joke someone else made quietly.
  13. Never have I ever watched the ending of a series before the person I was watching it with.

Rules

  • Everyone holds up ten fingers (or takes ten tokens: coins, pasta, grapes).
  • Draw a prompt and read it out loud. Whoever has done it puts one finger down.
  • Anyone who puts a finger down may be asked for the story. Keep it to one minute.
  • If nobody puts a finger down, the reader loses one instead, for picking a boring one.
  • The first person with no fingers left is crowned the most experienced in the room.
  • Short version: play to five fingers.
