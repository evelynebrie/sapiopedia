60 truth or dare questions (clean, funny, for adults)

30 truths and 30 dares you can actually do in a living room. Draw one at random, or scroll the full list.

Truth or dare works best when the truths are revealing without being cruel and the dares are silly without being dangerous. These 60 prompts were written for mixed groups of adults: nothing here requires drinking, leaving the flat, or embarrassing anyone for real. Press Truth or Dare and let fate decide.

Press the button to draw a question.

Truths: soft

  1. What is the most useless skill you are secretly proud of?
  2. Which song do you know every word to but would never admit to liking?
  3. What did you want to be when you were seven?
  4. What is the last thing you searched for online?
  5. Which app would be hardest for you to give up for a month?
  6. What is the strangest thing you have eaten and pretended to enjoy?
  7. What is your go-to move when you forget someone's name?
  8. Which chore do you avoid until it becomes a crisis?
  9. What is the pettiest reason you have ever disliked someone?
  10. What is one thing in this room you would take home if nobody noticed?
  11. What was your most embarrassing fashion phase?
  12. What is the longest you have gone without washing your hair?
  13. Which fictional character do you think would be your best friend?
  14. What is a food everyone loves that you quietly can't stand?
  15. What is the most childish thing you still do when nobody is watching?

Truths: spicy but clean

  1. Who in this room would you call first if you got arrested, and why them?
  2. What is the biggest lie you told your parents that they still believe?
  3. Have you ever pretended to be sick to skip something? What was it?
  4. What is the most money you have spent on something ridiculous?
  5. Which of your friends' habits annoys you the most, without naming them?
  6. What is a compliment you received that you still think about?
  7. Have you ever snooped through someone's phone or bathroom cabinet?
  8. What is the worst gift you have ever given, and did they find out?
  9. What do you do that you would hate to be filmed doing?
  10. Have you ever re-gifted something to the person who gave it to you?
  11. What is the longest you have held a grudge, and over what?
  12. What is one opinion you keep to yourself to avoid arguments?
  13. What was your most awkward first impression on someone here tonight?
  14. Which text message are you most relieved you never sent?
  15. What is the worst thing you ever got away with at school or work?

Dares: soft

  1. Speak in a whisper until your next turn.
  2. Do your best impression of someone in the room, and let them guess who.
  3. Let the group pick a new ringtone for your phone for the rest of the evening.
  4. Describe a random object in the room like a nature documentary narrator.
  5. Sing the chorus of the last song you listened to.
  6. Balance a book on your head while walking across the room.
  7. Talk in rhymes for the next two minutes.
  8. Show the group the last photo in your camera roll (you may skip once).
  9. Invent a secret handshake with the person on your left.
  10. Say the alphabet backwards without stopping.
  11. Compliment every person in the room in under 30 seconds.
  12. Do ten squats while naming ten animals.
  13. Draw a portrait of someone in the room in 20 seconds, using your non-dominant hand.
  14. Read the last text you received in a dramatic theatre voice.
  15. Do a slow-motion replay of how you entered the room tonight.

Dares: spicy but clean

  1. Call a friend and tell them, with total seriousness, that you have decided to become a professional mime.
  2. Let someone else post a story from your phone. They write it, you approve nothing.
  3. Eat a spoonful of a condiment chosen by the group.
  4. Swap an item of clothing (jacket, socks, hat) with someone for the rest of the game.
  5. Perform a 30-second interpretive dance of your morning routine.
  6. Type "I secretly" into your phone and read the keyboard's suggestions out loud.
  7. Hold a plank while answering three rapid-fire questions from the group.
  8. Speak only in questions until your next turn.
  9. Give a two-minute TED talk on a topic the group chooses right now.
  10. Let someone give you a new hairstyle using only what is in the room.
  11. Text your most recent contact "I have a confession" and wait 60 seconds before explaining.
  12. Act out a movie scene with no sound until someone guesses the film.
  13. Wear oven mitts for the next three rounds, including when using your phone.
  14. Let the group choose your profile photo for the next 24 hours.
  15. Record a 15-second voice memo rapping about the person on your right, and play it to the group.

Rules

  • Sit in a circle. The youngest player starts and chooses who goes next.
  • The chosen player picks truth or dare, then draws a prompt with the button above.
  • A truth must be answered honestly, in at least two sentences. No one-word answers.
  • A dare must be completed within two minutes. Each player gets one skip for the whole evening.
  • Nobody may pick the same option three times in a row.
  • The game ends when everyone has had at least three turns, or when the snacks run out.
