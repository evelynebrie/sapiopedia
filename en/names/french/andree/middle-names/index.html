Questions

What is a good middle name for Andrée?

Names with a different number of syllables flow best with Andrée, for example Marguerite, Mauricette, Cathérine.

What sibling names go with Andrée?

For a sister, Lucienne or Raymonde; for a brother, Lucien or Henri: the same style, but a different first letter and ending.

Does Andrée work as a middle name?

Yes. With 2 syllables, Andrée sits best after a first name with 1 or 3 syllables.
