Questions

What is a good middle name for Aurélie?

Names with a different number of syllables flow best with Aurélie, for example Martine, Christiane, Cécile.

What sibling names go with Aurélie?

For a sister, Laétitia or Claire; for a brother, Vincent or Benoît: the same style, but a different first letter and ending.

Does Aurélie work as a middle name?

Yes. With 3 syllables, Aurélie sits best after a first name with 2 syllables.
