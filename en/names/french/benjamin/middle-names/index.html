Questions

What is a good middle name for Benjamin?

Names with a different number of syllables flow best with Benjamin, for example Michel, Thomas, Jordan.

What sibling names go with Benjamin?

For a sister, Laura or Sarah; for a brother, Simon or Jason: the same style, but a different first letter and ending.

Does Benjamin work as a middle name?

Yes. With 3 syllables, Benjamin sits best after a first name with 2 syllables.
