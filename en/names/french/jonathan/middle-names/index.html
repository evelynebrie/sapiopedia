Questions

What is a good middle name for Jonathan?

Names with a different number of syllables flow best with Jonathan, for example Michel, Antoine, Benoît.

What sibling names go with Jonathan?

For a sister, Lætitia or Myriam; for a brother, Manuel or Benoît: the same style, but a different first letter and ending.

Does Jonathan work as a middle name?

Yes. With 3 syllables, Jonathan sits best after a first name with 2 syllables.
