Questions

What is a good middle name for Louis?

Names with a different number of syllables flow best with Louis, for example Alexandre, Olivier, Antoine.

What sibling names go with Louis?

For a sister, Jeanne or Marie; for a brother, Joseph or Charles: the same style, but a different first letter and ending.

Does Louis work as a middle name?

Yes. With 1 syllable, Louis sits best after a first name with 2 syllables.
