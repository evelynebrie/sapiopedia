Thérèse

\te.ʁɛz\♀French girl's name

A French girl's name of Spanish origin.

At a glance

GenderFeminine
OriginSpanish
Pronunciation\te.ʁɛz\
Also spelledTeresa, Thérèze
Compound namesAnne-Thérèse, Marie-Thérèse, Thérèse-Marie, Marie-Thérèze, Thérèze-Marie

Origin and meaning

First recorded as the name of a fourth-century Spanish saint. Of obscure origin; suggestions include Therasia or Thera, ancient name of the Greek island Thira.
