Gender-neutral names with origins and meanings

25 unisex names used for boys and girls, from Charlie to Rowan, with origin and meaning and advice on choosing one.

A gender-neutral name is one that is given to boys and girls in meaningful numbers, not merely one that could be. The 25 below qualify in at least one English- or French-speaking country, and several (Camille, Dominique, Claude) have been truly shared in French for a century or more.

How to choose

  • Look at how the name leans where you live: Robin is mostly male in France and mostly female in the United States, and Andrea flips entirely between Italy and English-speaking countries.
  • Decide whether you want a name that is neutral by sound (Sacha, Eden) or a short form that stays open (Alex, Charlie, Lou).
  • Consider the surname together with the first name; a neutral first name next to a surname that is also a first name (Morgan Taylor) can confuse forms and introductions.

Meanings follow standard etymological dictionaries of given names; where scholars disagree, we say so. Popularity varies a great deal by country and by decade.
