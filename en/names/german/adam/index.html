Adam

[ˈaːdam]♂German boy's name

A German boy's name of Latin origin.

At a glance

GenderMasculine
OriginLatin
Pronunciation[ˈaːdam]

Origin and meaning

Borrowed from Latin Adam, from Ancient Greek Ἀδάμ (Adám), from Hebrew אָדָם (ʾāḏām, “man, soil, light brown”).
