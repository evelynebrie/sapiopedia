Questions

What is a good middle name for Arthur?

Names with a different number of syllables flow best with Arthur, for example Ernst, Adalbert, Geert.

What sibling names go with Arthur?

For a sister, Jennifer or Brigitte; for a brother, Dylan or Tristan: the same style, but a different first letter and ending.

Does Arthur work as a middle name?

Yes. With 2 syllables, Arthur sits best after a first name with 1 or 3 syllables.
