Questions

What is a good middle name for Ernst?

Names with a different number of syllables flow best with Ernst, for example Wilhelm, Adolf, Gerhard.

What sibling names go with Ernst?

For a sister, Irma or Gertrud; for a brother, Karl or Walter: the same style, but a different first letter and ending.

Does Ernst work as a middle name?

Yes. With 1 syllable, Ernst sits best after a first name with 2 syllables.
