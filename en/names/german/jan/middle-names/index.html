Questions

What is a good middle name for Jan?

Names with a different number of syllables flow best with Jan, for example David, Joseph, Maurice.

What sibling names go with Jan?

For a sister, Marthe or Michelle; for a brother, Tim or Roland: the same style, but a different first letter and ending.

Does Jan work as a middle name?

Yes. With 1 syllable, Jan sits best after a first name with 2 syllables.
