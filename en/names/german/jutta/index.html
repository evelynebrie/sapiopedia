Jutta

[ˈjʊta]♀German girl's name

A German girl's name of Germanic origin.

At a glance

GenderFeminine
OriginGermanic
Pronunciation[ˈjʊta]
Related formsJudith, Jutte, Ita, Ite, Itta, Itte

Origin and meaning

Perhaps from a Middle Low German diminutive of Judith, from Hebrew יְהוּדִית (yəhūḏīṯ, “Judith”). A Germanic origin has also been suggested. Compare Danish Jytte.
