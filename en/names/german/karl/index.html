Karl

[kaʁl]♂German boy's name

A German boy's name of Old High German origin.

At a glance

GenderMasculine
OriginOld High German
Pronunciation[kaʁl]
Also spelledCarl
Short and pet formsKalle, Kalli
Feminine formKarla, Karola, Carola, Caroline

Origin and meaning

From Old High German karal, from Proto-Germanic *karlaz (“free man”), *karilaz (“man, elder”). Cognate with French and English Charles.
