Questions

What is a good middle name for Philipp?

Names with a different number of syllables flow best with Philipp, for example Dominik, Zacharias, Jakobus.

What sibling names go with Philipp?

For a sister, Helene or Agnes; for a brother, Alexander or Immanuel: the same style, but a different first letter and ending.

Does Philipp work as a middle name?

Yes. With 2 syllables, Philipp sits best after a first name with 1 or 3 syllables.
