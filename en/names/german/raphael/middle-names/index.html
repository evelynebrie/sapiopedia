Questions

What is a good middle name for Raphael?

Names with a different number of syllables flow best with Raphael, for example Johannes, Jeremia, Nehemia.

What sibling names go with Raphael?

For a sister, Elisabeth or Eva; for a brother, Benjamin or Josua: the same style, but a different first letter and ending.

Does Raphael work as a middle name?

Yes. With 2 syllables, Raphael sits best after a first name with 1 or 3 syllables.
