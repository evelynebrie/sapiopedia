Questions

What is a good middle name for Sarah?

Names with a different number of syllables flow best with Sarah, for example Susanne, Irene, Isabel.

What sibling names go with Sarah?

For a sister, Ruth or Eva; for a brother, Abraham or Daniel: the same style, but a different first letter and ending.

Does Sarah work as a middle name?

Yes. With 2 syllables, Sarah sits best after a first name with 1 or 3 syllables.
