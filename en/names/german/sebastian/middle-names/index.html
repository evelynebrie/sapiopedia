What is a good middle name for Sebastian?

Names with a different number of syllables flow best with Sebastian, for example Viktor, Oskar, Daniel.

What sibling names go with Sebastian?

For a sister, Johanna or Helena; for a brother, Gregor or Lukas: the same style, but a different first letter and ending.

Does Sebastian work as a middle name?

Yes. With 3 syllables, Sebastian sits best after a first name with 2 syllables.
