Names that work in both French and English

25 first names spelt almost identically and understood instantly in French and English, from Chloé to Victor, with meanings.

A truly bilingual name is written the same (or nearly) in both languages, is pronounced recognisably in each, and does not turn into a different word on the other side. The 25 below pass that test; where French uses an accent, we show both spellings.

How to choose

  • Say it in both accents. Thomas, Simon and Vincent survive the switch; names with a nasal vowel or a silent ending in French (Jean, Hugues) do not.
  • Choose your spelling once and keep it: Zoé with the accent will lose it on many English forms, Zoe without it will gain one in France.
  • Mind false friends: Jules, Guy and Jean are everyday French names that read quite differently to an English ear.

Meanings follow standard etymological dictionaries of given names; where scholars disagree, we say so. Popularity varies a great deal by country and by decade.
