Nature-inspired names: flowers, trees, sea and sky

25 names drawn from flowers, trees, stones, the sea and the sky, from Hazel to Océane, with origin and meaning.

Nature names are among the oldest there are: Rose and Lily have been given for centuries, and Sylvie and Flora go back to Rome. The 25 below mix English word-names (Willow, Reed), French favourites (Océane, Aurore) and a few whose link to nature hides behind an older language (Iris, Arthur).

How to choose

  • Think about the plant or element itself: a child named Ivy or Willow will hear comparisons to it for life, so pick one whose image you like.
  • Word-names (Fern, Reed, Ash) read as modern in English but may sound unusual in French; names with a Latin root (Sylvie, Stella, Marine) cross the border more easily.
  • Pair carefully with the surname: Rose Bush and Coral Reef are real pitfalls, and friends will notice before you do.

Meanings follow standard etymological dictionaries of given names; where scholars disagree, we say so. Popularity varies a great deal by country and by decade.
