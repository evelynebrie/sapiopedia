Rare and soft girl names with meanings

25 uncommon, gentle-sounding girl names with origin and meaning, from Elowen to Sidonie, plus tips for choosing one.

A soft name usually has open vowels, few hard consonants and two or three syllables, and a rare one is simply a name you will not hear three times in the same playground. The 25 below sit at that intersection: well attested, easy to say, and still uncommon in most English- and French-speaking countries.

How to choose

  • Say the full name out loud with your surname; a soft first name next to a soft surname can blur together, and a crisp surname often sets it off better.
  • Check the spelling you will have to repeat: Maëlle and Elowen are lovely but will be misspelt; decide whether you mind.
  • Rare today is not rare forever. If avoiding a trend matters to you, favour names with no celebrity or television attached to them yet.

Meanings follow standard etymological dictionaries of given names; where scholars disagree, we say so. Popularity varies a great deal by country and by decade.
