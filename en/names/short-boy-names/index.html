Short boy names (3 to 4 letters) with meanings

25 boy names of three or four letters, from Leo to Ezra, with origin and meaning and three tips for choosing a short name.

Short names are easy to call across a playground, hard to misspell and almost impossible to shorten further, which is why so many parents land on them. The 25 below have three or four letters each and come from Hebrew, Latin, Celtic, Germanic, Arabic and Scandinavian traditions.

How to choose

  • Balance the rhythm: a one-syllable first name (Max, Finn) sounds best with a longer surname, while Hugo or Enzo can carry a short one.
  • Decide whether you want a stand-alone name (Finn, Otto) or a short form that leaves a longer option open (Sam for Samuel, Theo for Theodore).
  • Check how the name is pronounced in the languages your child will grow up with; Kai, Ian and Luca each shift noticeably between English and French.

Meanings follow standard etymological dictionaries of given names; where scholars disagree, we say so. Popularity varies a great deal by country and by decade.
