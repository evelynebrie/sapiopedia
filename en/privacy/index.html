Privacy policy

Sapiopedia does not require an account and does not collect personal information. Quiz and calculator inputs are processed in your browser and are not sent to our servers.

You can opt out of personalised advertising in your Google Ads settings. Visitors in the EU/EEA and UK will be asked for consent before any advertising cookies are set.
