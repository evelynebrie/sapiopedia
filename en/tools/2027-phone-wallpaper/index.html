How it works

Everything is drawn in your browser at the exact resolution of your phone, so the wallpaper stays sharp. Nothing is sent anywhere: your goals stay on your phone. The top third of the image is left clear for the clock and the date; tick the box under the preview to see where they will sit.

To change it later, tap Copy edit link: the link keeps your words, goals and style, and opens the editor exactly as you left it.
