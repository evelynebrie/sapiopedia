Baby age calculator: weeks, months and corrected age

Enter your baby's birth date to get the age in weeks, days and months, plus the corrected age for premature babies.

Pick the date of birth.

Weeks and days are counted from the birth date to today; months follow the calendar (a baby born on the 15th turns one month old on the 15th of the next month). If you enter a due date earlier than today and later than the birth, we also show the corrected age, counted from the due date instead of the birth date: this is the age paediatricians use to follow the development of premature babies, usually until about 2 years.

Corrected (adjusted) age = chronological age minus the weeks born before the due date, as used by paediatric follow-up services. For any concern about growth or development, ask your doctor or midwife.
