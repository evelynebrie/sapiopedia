Birthday converter: days, weeks, months and full moons

Enter your date of birth and see how many days, weeks, months, full moons and heartbeats you have lived.

Pick your date of birth.

Days, weeks and months are counted from your birthday to today. Full moons use the mean synodic month (29.53 days); heartbeats assume an average 72 beats per minute; the distance uses Earth's mean orbital speed of 29.8 km/s. Everything is computed in your browser and nothing is sent anywhere.
