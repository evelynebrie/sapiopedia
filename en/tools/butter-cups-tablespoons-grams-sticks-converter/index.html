Butter: cups, tablespoons, grams, sticks converter

Convert butter between cups, tablespoons, grams and US sticks. 1 cup = 227 g, 1 tbsp = 14.2 g, 1 stick = 113 g.

Type an amount in any field; the others fill in.

Butter is dense and barely compressible, so volume-to-weight conversion is more reliable than for flour or sugar. We use the standard figures: 1 US cup = 227 g, 1 tablespoon = 14.2 g, and 1 US stick (½ cup, 8 tablespoons) = 113 g. European butter is sold by weight, usually in 250 g blocks, so 1 cup is just under a full block.

Assumptions: USDA FoodData Central density for butter (227 g per US cup); US stick = 113 g.
