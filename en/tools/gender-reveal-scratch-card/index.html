How it works

  1. Pick the answer above: the confetti turns pink or blue to match. Or write your own.
  2. Add who it's for and a message: the due date, the name if you've chosen it.
  3. Create the link and send it, or open it at the party and hand the phone to the guest of honour.

Ideas for the big moment

At a party
Open the link on a TV or a tablet and let the grandparents scratch it in front of everyone.
At a distance
Send one card to each family and ask them to film the moment they scratch it.
If you don't know yet yourselves
Give the envelope from the ultrasound to a friend. They make the card and send you the link, and you find out together.
