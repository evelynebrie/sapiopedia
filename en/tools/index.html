Tools

Calculators and converters that answer one question each

Flour: cups to grams converter

Convert cups of all-purpose flour to grams and back. 1 cup = 125 g.

Birthday converter: days, weeks, months and full moons

Enter your date of birth and see how many days, weeks, months, full moons and heartbeats you have lived.

Butter: cups, tablespoons, grams, sticks converter

Convert butter between cups, tablespoons, grams and US sticks. 1 cup = 227 g, 1 tbsp = 14.2 g, 1 stick = 113 g.

Sugar: cups to grams converter (white, brown, icing)

Convert cups of granulated, brown or icing sugar to grams and back. 1 cup white sugar = 200 g, brown 220 g, icing 120 g.

Oven temperature converter: °F, °C and gas mark

Convert oven temperatures between Fahrenheit, Celsius and British gas marks, with a full reference table.

Baby age calculator: weeks, months and corrected age

Enter your baby's birth date to get the age in weeks, days and months, plus the corrected age for premature babies.

Pregnancy due date calculator (from your last period)

Enter the first day of your last period to get your due date (Naegele's rule, 280 days) and your current week of pregnancy.

Sleep calculator: when to go to bed to wake up refreshed

Choose your wake-up time and get bedtimes based on 90-minute sleep cycles, plus 15 minutes to fall asleep.

Paint calculator: how many litres for your walls

Enter the wall area (m² or ft²), coats and coverage to get the litres of paint needed, minus doors and windows.

Tip and bill split calculator

Enter the bill, tip percentage and number of people to get the tip, the total and each person's share. Presets 15, 18, 20 %.

Feng Shui Room Planner

Draw your room, add your door, windows and furniture, and see where each piece works best according to feng shui.
