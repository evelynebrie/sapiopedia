Oven temperature converter: °F, °C and gas mark

Convert oven temperatures between Fahrenheit, Celsius and British gas marks, with a full reference table.

Type a temperature in °F or °C, or a gas mark.

The conversion is the exact formula °C = (°F − 32) × 5⁄9, rounded to the nearest degree. Gas marks are a British scale with no formula behind it: each mark corresponds to a conventional temperature, so we show the nearest mark. Recipes usually round oven temperatures anyway: 350 °F is quoted as 180 °C, not 177 °C. For a fan (convection) oven, most manufacturers suggest lowering the temperature by about 20 °C (25 °F).
