Paint calculator: how many litres for your walls

Enter the wall area (m² or ft²), coats and coverage to get the litres of paint needed, minus doors and windows.

Enter the wall area to paint.

Litres = (wall area − doors and windows) × number of coats ÷ coverage. Wall area is simply the perimeter of the room multiplied by the height of the walls. A standard door is about 2 m² (20 ft²) and an average window 1.5 m² (16 ft²). Coverage depends on the paint and the surface: 10 m²/L is typical for emulsion on a smooth, primed wall; fresh plaster or textured surfaces absorb more, so check the figure on the tin. The “buy” figure adds 10 % for touch-ups and roller losses, rounded up to the half-litre.

Default coverage of 10 m² per litre per coat, the usual figure quoted on interior emulsion tins (manufacturers state 8–14 m²/L).
