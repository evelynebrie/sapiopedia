How it works

  1. Write who the card is for and your news. Pick an idea above or write your own.
  2. Add a detail if you like: the due date, a first ultrasound, a nickname for the baby.
  3. Create the link and send it. They scratch the grey card on their phone, and the confetti starts as the news appears.

Wording ideas

For future grandparents
You're going to be grandparents! · Your new title: Grandma, starting in June · Clear your calendar for babysitting
For a brother or sister
You're going to be an aunt! · Promoted to uncle · Someone small will call you Auntie soon
For an older child
You're going to be a big sister! · Big brother, reporting for duty in June
For your partner
You're going to be a dad! · We're having a baby! · Two pink lines this morning

If you are announcing to several people, make one card each: everyone sees their own name, and you can watch each reaction.
