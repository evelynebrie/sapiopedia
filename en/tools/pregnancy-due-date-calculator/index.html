Pregnancy due date calculator (from your last period)

Enter the first day of your last period to get your due date (Naegele's rule, 280 days) and your current week of pregnancy.

Pick the first day of your last period.

This is Naegele's rule, the method used on most pregnancy wheels: the due date is 280 days (40 weeks) after the first day of the last menstrual period. It assumes a regular 28-day cycle with ovulation on day 14. Only about 4 % of babies arrive on the exact date; most are born within two weeks either side. An early ultrasound often gives a more precise date, and your doctor or midwife may adjust it.
