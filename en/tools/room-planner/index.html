      How the room planner works

      Type the exact size of your room, or pick a shape and drag its corners. Add the door and windows, then the furniture: each piece is drawn to a typical size and lands on the best free spot.

      Select a piece to see a measuring line from its front to the nearest wall or piece of furniture. It is rated fits when at least 60 cm is free, tight between 30 and 60 cm, and blocked when it overlaps something, leaves less than 30 cm, or sits where the door swings open. Planners usually allow 60 cm to walk past furniture and 90 cm for a comfortable main path.

      The Floor and fit panel adds up the floor area and how much of it the furniture covers. Your layout is saved in your browser only. Want to know what the arrangement says about energy and sleep? Try the Feng Shui Room Planner.
