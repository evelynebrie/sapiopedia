Sleep calculator: when to go to bed to wake up refreshed

Choose your wake-up time and get bedtimes based on 90-minute sleep cycles, plus 15 minutes to fall asleep.

Pick a wake-up time.

A night's sleep runs in cycles of roughly 90 minutes, from light sleep through deep sleep to REM. Waking at the end of a cycle, rather than in the middle of deep sleep, usually feels easier. The calculator counts back from your alarm: 15 minutes to fall asleep, then 6, 5 or 4 full cycles (9, 7½ or 6 hours of sleep). Most adults need 7 to 9 hours, so the first two lines are the ones to aim for.

Assumptions: 90-minute average sleep cycle and 15 minutes to fall asleep (textbook averages; real cycles vary between 70 and 120 minutes). Adult sleep-duration range from the National Sleep Foundation.
