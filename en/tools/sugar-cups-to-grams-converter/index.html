Sugar: cups to grams converter (white, brown, icing)

Convert cups of granulated, brown or icing sugar to grams and back. 1 cup white sugar = 200 g, brown 220 g, icing 120 g.

Choose a sugar, then type cups or grams.

Sugars differ a lot by volume. Granulated sugar packs tightly and weighs about 200 g per US cup; brown sugar is measured packed in the cup and reaches about 220 g; icing sugar is airy and comes to only about 120 g. Changing the type in the menu recalculates the result instantly.

Densities from USDA FoodData Central (granulated 200 g, brown packed 220 g, powdered 120 g per US cup).
