Tip and bill split calculator

Enter the bill, tip percentage and number of people to get the tip, the total and each person's share. Presets 15, 18, 20 %.

Enter the bill amount.

Tip = amount × percentage; the total is the bill plus the tip, divided equally between everyone at the table. In the United States 15–20 % is standard for table service. In Quebec and the rest of Canada the custom is to tip on the amount before taxes: in Quebec the two sales taxes add up to just under 15 %, which is why many people simply tip the tax amount. Enter the pre-tax figure in the optional field and the tip will be calculated on it while the total still uses the full bill.

Percentages are customs, not rules; adjust to local practice and the service you received.
